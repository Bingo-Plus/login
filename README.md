## Safe Login Checklist

Before entering account information:

1. Verify the full BingoPlus domain.
2. Avoid copied login pages or unofficial mirrors.
3. Keep passwords and OTPs private.
4. Use current account-access instructions.
5. Use official recovery options if you cannot sign in.

## Registration

New users should follow the current registration instructions shown on the official BingoPlus account-access page:

https://bingoplus.com/play/login-register/

Registration requirements and account-verification steps may change over time.

## Password and Account Recovery

If you cannot access your account:

- Use the official recovery process.
- Do not share passwords or OTPs with third parties.
- Avoid unofficial account-recovery services.
- Review the current support instructions.

## Account Safety

A familiar logo or page design does not prove that a login page is legitimate.

Always check the full website address before entering a mobile number, password, OTP or other account information.
